The organism is a self-feeding and self-repair- ing machine, but it cannot do its repairs when it is in full work ; it must have for its parts, as for its whole, its recurring periods of adequate rest ; and the time comes at last when, like any other machine, it wears out, is no more capable of repair, and when the exhaustion which ensues is death — the sleep during which there is no repair and from which there is no awaking.
